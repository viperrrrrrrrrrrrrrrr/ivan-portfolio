Brasswood Barber Co. — online booking web app for a barbershop

An online booking web app for a (fictional) barbershop: clients book a service with their preferred barber in a few taps, and the team runs the day from an admin panel — schedule, bookings, clients, services and barbers.

brasswood-booking.pages.dev

For clients

  • Step-by-step booking — service, barber, date and a free time slot, then contact details and a confirmation.
  • Manage a booking — clients can look up, cancel or reschedule their booking.
  • Salon time zone — slots always follow the shop’s time zone (America/New_York), whatever time zone the visitor’s browser is in.
  • Responsive design — works on phones, tablets and desktops.

For the team — admin panel

  • Schedule — day and week views of all bookings.
  • Bookings & clients — every booking and client in one place.
  • Services & barbers — editable by the team.

Modern front end

React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS.

Server version

Cloudflare Pages Functions with a Cloudflare D1 database, server-side admin authentication and sessions.

No double bookings

Verified by a test: 10 parallel bookings of the same slot result in exactly 1 success.

Automated tests

Unit and end-to-end tests cover the booking logic and the main user flows.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Cloudflare Pages Functions
  • Cloudflare D1

Process

Simple, transparent, fast

No jargon and no surprises — you always know what’s happening and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Project brief

    You tell me about your business, your customers and what the site needs to achieve. Everything happens in writing, by email or chat, so you can answer whenever it suits you.

  2. 02

    Plan & fixed quote

    You get a clear page structure, timeline and fixed price before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    I build the site and share a live preview link, so you can review it on your own phone and request changes.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    I publish the site on your domain, connect the contact form and show you how everything works.
